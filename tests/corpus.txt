# The models of the tlaplus/Examples corpus that scripts/corpus.sh cross-checks (see "The corpus" in
# CLAUDE.md). Every model of the manifests (specifications/*/manifest.json) at the pinned commit is
# listed once, by its module and its cfg:
#   check <module> <model> [rules=...]     selected; tla2rs must match TLC
#   gap   <module> <model> [rules=...] [norun] # reason   selected; a known difference, with its measured
#                                          reason (norun: too costly to run until it is fixed)
#   skip  <module> <model> # reason        not selected, and why
# Selected are the exhaustive searches that TLC 2.19 finishes, with only tla2tools.jar, in 60 s or less
# with one worker (with -dump dot, as expected.sh runs it). `rules=` names narrowing rules of
# crosscheck.sh (distinct-only, exact, ignore-exit, error-lines), as `\* crosscheck:` lines do in a cfg.
