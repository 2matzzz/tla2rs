# Intentional differences from TLC: where TLC is wrong, tla2rs does not copy it (README.md, "Intentional
# differences"; CLAUDE.md, "Intentional differences" says how they are tested). One line per difference:
#   <name> <fields of the summary that may differ from TLC's, comma-separated> # what TLC does and why
# The fields are verdict, generated, distinct, queue, trace, states, edges, dump, messages, errors,
# userfile and exit; every other field must still equal TLC's.
tlcget-rebuild generated,distinct,queue,trace,states,edges,messages,errors,exit # TLC cannot rebuild a behavior when TLCGet registers make Init or the actions yield other states than the search found (its own "probably a TLC bug", or a cut-short behavior and, with -continue, a NullPointerException); tla2rs shows the behavior the search found
error-reentry trace,messages,errors,exit # After an invariant fails to evaluate, TLC's exit code depends on its own re-entry: with -continue it reports the error again as an unexpected exception and exits with 255, and when the pass it makes again over the successors meets a second such failure it exits with 255 (with -continue also reporting that failure twice, each with its behavior); tla2rs reports the error that stopped the search once, with exit 76, and keeps that pass's printed lines and counts
